#### 3. Expanded FAQs (20+ Questions)

**Registration Process:**
1. What is the minimum corpus requirement for AIF?
   - Category I & II: ₹20 crores
   - Category III: ₹20 crores
   
2. Can foreign entities sponsor an AIF in India?
   - Yes, with RBI/FEMA compliance

3. What is the typical timeline for AIF registration?
   - 8-12 weeks from complete application

4. Can an AIF have multiple schemes?
   - Yes, subject to SEBI approval

5. What are the key sponsor eligibility criteria?
   - Net worth of ₹10 crores
   - Track record of 5 years in fund management or 10 years in financial services

**Compliance & Operations:**
6. What is the lock-in period for AIF investments?
   - Category I & II: 3 years minimum
   - Category III: No mandatory lock-in

7. Can AIFs invest in listed securities?
   - Category I: Limited
   - Category II: Yes, with restrictions
   - Category III: Yes, including derivatives

8. What is the maximum number of investors allowed?
   - 1,000 investors per scheme

9. What is the minimum investment per investor?
   - ₹1 crore (₹25 lakhs for employees/directors)

10. Are AIFs required to appoint a custodian?
    - Yes, SEBI-registered custodian mandatory

**Tax & Structuring:**
11. What is the tax treatment for AIF investors?
    - Pass-through status for Category I & II
    - Category III taxed at fund level

12. Can AIFs be structured as trusts or companies?
    - Trust, LLP, or Company structure allowed

13. What are the leverage limits for AIFs?
    - Category I: No leverage
    - Category II: Limited to 2x NAV
    - Category III: Up to 2x NAV

**Reporting & Disclosures:**
14. What are the reporting requirements?
    - Quarterly reports to SEBI
    - Annual audited financials
    - Valuation reports

15. Can AIFs charge performance fees?
    - Yes, subject to high-water mark principle

**Modifications & Exits:**
16. Can the investment strategy be changed post-registration?
    - Requires SEBI approval and investor consent

17. What is the process for winding up an AIF?
    - Investor approval + SEBI notification
    - Asset liquidation and distribution

18. Can an AIF scheme be extended beyond tenure?
    - Yes, with investor approval

**International Aspects:**
19. Can AIFs invest in overseas securities?
    - Yes, within RBI's overseas investment limits

20. Can NRIs/FPIs invest in Indian AIFs?
    - Yes, subject to FEMA regulations

#### 4. Common Pitfalls Section
- Inadequate sponsor track record

- Non-compliant fee structure
- Missing risk disclosures
- Inadequate compliance framework

#### 5. Post-Registration Checklist

- Finalize PPM
- Set up compliance systems
- Onboard custodian
- Begin fundraising
