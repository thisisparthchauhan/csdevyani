**Three pillars**:
1. **Educational & Certification Requirements**

   - Mandatory NISM Research Analyst certification
   - Continuous certification validity

2. **Fit and Proper Criteria**
   - Integrity and reputation
   - Regulatory track record
   - Absence of fraud or moral turpitude
   - Financial soundness

3. **Infrastructure & Operational Readiness**
   - Adequate workspace and IT systems
   - Secure data storage
   - Record-keeping capabilities
   - Compliance oversight

### 7. Registration Process Section
**5-Step Process**:
1. **Structuring the Research Model**
   - Nature of services, entity structure, revenue model, independence

2. **Documentation & Internal Framework**
   - Application forms, certifications, internal policies, infrastructure details

3. **Application to SEBI**
   - Fees, supporting documents, clarifications

4. **Regulatory Scrutiny**
   - Eligibility evaluation, independence check, operational readiness

5. **Grant of Certificate**
   - Certificate issuance, terms and conditions

- Application Fee (non-refundable)
