**Key Message**:

### 3. Code of Conduct & Ethical Obligations
**4 Core Principles**:
1. **Honesty & Fairness** - Acting honestly in research preparation and dissemination
2. **Objectivity & Independence** - Ensuring independence of opinions
3. **Avoid Misleading Content** - No exaggerated or selective recommendations
4. **Due Diligence** - Exercise diligence before issuing research

**Key Principle**: Research must be driven by analysis, not incentives

### 4. Independence of Research & Conflict Management
**Independence Requirements**:
- Complete independence between research and commercial activities
- No influence from issuers, promoters, or intermediaries
- Clear identification and management of conflicts

**3-Step Conflict Management Process**:
1. Identified in advance
2. Disclosed prominently
3. Managed through internal controls

**Critical Point**: Failure to disclose conflicts = serious compliance breach

### 5. Mandatory Disclosures in Research Reports
**5 Required Disclosures**:
1. Research Analyst registration number
2. Name of analyst or entity
3. Nature of recommendation (buy/sell/hold/view)
4. Time horizon and risks
5. Disclosure of personal or institutional interest

**Important**: Disclosures must be clear, legible, not buried in fine print

### 6. Trading Restrictions & Personal Dealings
**4 Strict Restrictions**:
1. **No Pre-Publication Trading** - Cannot trade before research publication
2. **Cooling-Off Periods** - Mandatory waiting periods before/after recommendations
3. **Pre-Clearance Required** - Internal approvals for personal trades
4. **Documentation Mandatory** - All trades must be documented

**Purpose**: Prevent front-running and information misuse

### 7. Maintenance of Records & Documentation
**Record Retention Table** (All 5 years minimum):
- Research reports & rationale
- Data sources & analysis notes
- Disclosures & disclaimers
- Client communication
- Personal trading records
- Complaints & grievance records

**Note**: Electronic records allowed if secure, retrievable, and tamper-proof

### 8. Client Onboarding & Communication Controls
**4 Key Controls**:
1. **Client Documentation** - Clear onboarding documentation
2. **Scope Definition** - Define scope of research services
3. **No Return Assurance** - Cannot guarantee returns
4. **Consistent Communication** - Must align with published research

**Prohibition**: Selective sharing of recommendations strictly prohibited

### 9. Website & Digital Platform Compliance
**4 Requirements for Digital Platforms**:
1. Registration details prominently displayed
2. Disclosures visible on all platforms
3. Historical performance not misrepresented
4. Testimonials/guarantees not used improperly

**Key Principle**: Digital reach does not dilute regulatory responsibility

### 10. Advertisement & Public Communication Restrictions
**Research Analysts Must NOT**:
- Advertise guaranteed returns
- Use promotional language inconsistent with research
- Publish selective/cherry-picked performance results
- Mislead investors through marketing claims

**Standard**: All communication must be educational and factual, not promotional

### 11. Grievance Redressal Mechanism
**4 Required Elements**:
1. Formal grievance redressal mechanism
2. Designated contact details for complaints
3. Timely resolution of investor grievances
4. Maintenance of complaint registers

**Warning**: Unresolved complaints may escalate to SEBI and invite inspection

### 12. Periodic Reporting & Regulatory Intimations
**3 Reporting Requirements**:
1. Submit periodic compliance confirmations
2. Intimate changes in business model, control, or key personnel
3. Report material events impacting research independence

**Principle**: Timely reporting reflects regulatory maturity and governance strength

### 13. Annual Compliance Audit
**Who Conducts**:
- Practising Chartered Accountant (CA)
- Company Secretary (CS)
- Cost Accountant (CMA)

**What is Examined**:
- Adherence to SEBI Research Analyst Regulations
- Disclosure practices
- Trading restrictions
- Record maintenance
- Conflict management

**Action Required**: Audit observations must be addressed promptly

### 14. SEBI Inspection & Enforcement Readiness
**SEBI May Conduct Inspections**:
- With or without prior notice (surprise visits)
- Comprehensive coverage (records, systems, communications)
- Digital content included (archived material)

**Requirement**: Maintain inspection-ready documentation at all times

### 15. Consequences of Non-Compliance
**4 Major Consequences**:
1. **Monetary Penalties** - Significant fines
2. **Directions or Restrictions** - Operational restrictions
3. **Suspension or Cancellation** - Loss of registration
4. **Reputational Damage** - Loss of credibility and client trust

**Key Message**: Post-registration compliance is as critical as registration itself
