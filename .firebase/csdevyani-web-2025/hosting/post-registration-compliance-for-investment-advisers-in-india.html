Compliance is Continuous

Once Investment Adviser Registration in India is granted, the responsibility does not end with the certificate. Registered Investment Advisers are expected to operate as fiduciaries, with continuous compliance, documentation, and reporting obligations under the supervision of SEBI.

Failure to comply can result in inspections, penalties, or cancellation of registration.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQs – Post-Registration Compliance for Investment Advisers in India

1. What compliances are required after SEBI Investment Adviser registration?
After registration, Investment Advisers must comply with client onboarding norms, risk profiling, record maintenance, periodic reporting, annual compliance audits, and investor grievance redressal requirements as prescribed by SEBI.
2. Is annual compliance audit mandatory for Investment Advisers?
Yes, every registered Investment Adviser must conduct an annual compliance audit through a practising professional and submit the audit report within the prescribed timeline.
3. How long must client records be maintained?
Client KYC documents, risk profiling records, advisory rationale, agreements, and communication records must be preserved for at least five years.
4. Is risk profiling compulsory for every client?
Yes, risk profiling is mandatory before providing any personalised investment advice. Advice must always align with the client’s documented risk profile.
5. Is a written agreement required with clients?
Yes, Investment Advisers must execute a written agreement with each client defining scope of services, fees, disclosures, and grievance redressal mechanism.
6. Can an Investment Adviser earn commission from product distribution?
No, registered Investment Advisers are prohibited from earning commissions or engaging in distribution activities. Advisory and distribution must be completely segregated.
7. Are Investment Advisers required to maintain a website?
Yes, a functional website displaying registration details, disclosures, services offered, and grievance redressal information is mandatory.
8. What disclosures must be made to clients?
Investment Advisers must disclose their registration number, fee structure, scope of services, conflicts of interest, and limitations of advisory services.
9. Is registration on SEBI SCORES mandatory?
Yes, Investment Advisers must register on the SEBI SCORES platform to receive, track, and resolve investor complaints.
10. Who is responsible for compliance in a non-individual Investment Adviser?
The Principal Officer remains responsible for compliance, supported by a Compliance Officer or an independent compliance professional.
11. Are Investment Advisers subject to SEBI inspections?
Yes, SEBI may conduct inspections at any time to verify compliance, documentation, and adherence to regulatory requirements.
12. What happens if an Investment Adviser fails to comply with regulations?
Non-compliance may lead to penalties, directions, suspension, or cancellation of registration depending on the severity of violations.
13. Is client-level segregation mandatory?
Yes, advisory clients and distribution clients must be completely separate, with no overlap in revenue, referrals, or services.
14. Are Investment Advisers required to maintain compliance manuals?
Yes, maintaining documented policies and internal compliance procedures is strongly recommended and often reviewed during audits and inspections.
15. Can Investment Advisers use technology tools for advisory and compliance?
Yes, technology tools may be used, provided their scope, limitations, and role in advisory are transparently disclosed to clients.
16. Are Investment Advisers required to update client information periodically?
Yes, client risk profiles and financial information should be reviewed and updated periodically to ensure continued suitability of advice.
17. Is KYC compliance mandatory even for existing clients?
Yes, all clients must comply with KYC norms, including updated identity and financial information, irrespective of prior relationship.
18. Are fee disclosures mandatory in advisory agreements?
Yes, the fee structure, billing frequency, and mode of payment must be clearly disclosed in writing to clients.
19. Can Investment Advisers provide advice to overseas clients?
Investment Advisers may advise overseas clients, subject to compliance with applicable Indian regulations and foreign exchange laws.
20. How can Investment Advisers stay compliant on an ongoing basis?
Regular internal reviews, timely audits, proper documentation, updated disclosures, and continuous monitoring of regulatory changes are essential to remain compliant.
