Post-Registration Compliance

PMS Compliance
Obligations

Once Portfolio Manager Registration is granted, the entity becomes a continuously regulated fiduciary. SEBI’s supervision is ongoing, risk-based, and inspection-driven.

Get Compliance Support

Most regulatory actions in the PMS segment arise not at the entry stage, but due to post-registration lapses—especially in disclosures, reporting, client segregation, and governance.

Core Regulatory Obligations

Fiduciary Duty

Act in a fiduciary capacity; ensure fair, transparent, and non-discriminatory treatment.

Net Worth (₹5 Cr)

Maintain ₹5 Crore net worth continuously. Restore shortfall immediately.

Client Onboarding

Verify eligibility, execute written agreements, and document risk profiling.

Asset Segregation

Separate bank/demat accounts for each client. No pooling of funds.

Investment Discipline

Adhere to permitted universe. No speculative trades or deviations without consent.

Performance Reporting

Periodic reports with benchmark comparisons. No misleading disclosures.

Fees & Billing

Transparency in management/performance fees. No hidden charges.

Compliance Officer

Designated officer for monitoring compliance and timely filings.

SEBI Reporting

Submit periodic reports, net worth statements, and compliance confirmations.

Audits & Reviews

Annual statutory audit and periodic internal compliance reviews.

Conflict Management

Identify and manage conflicts. Avoid preferential treatment.

Grievance Redressal

Time-bound resolution of complaints with proper documentation.

PMS Compliance – FAQs

Expert answers to common compliance questions.

Is compliance mandatory after PMS registration?
Yes. PMS registration is subject to continuous compliance under SEBI regulations throughout the validity of registration.
Who is responsible for PMS compliance within the organisation?
The Compliance Officer is primarily responsible, with oversight from senior management and the Board.
Can PMS compliance be handled informally?
No. SEBI expects structured, documented, and auditable compliance processes.
Does SEBI monitor PMS operations regularly?
Yes. PMS entities are subject to inspections, reviews, and reporting obligations.
Is PMS compliance the same as advisory compliance?
No. PMS compliance is stricter because portfolio managers exercise discretionary control over client assets.
Is minimum net worth required after PMS registration?
Yes. A minimum net worth of ₹5 crore must be maintained at all times.
What happens if PMS net worth falls below the prescribed limit?
The deficiency must be restored immediately, failing which regulatory action may follow.
Is periodic net worth reporting mandatory?
Yes. PMS entities must prepare and maintain periodic net worth statements.
Can PMS operate while restoring net worth?
Operations may continue only if restoration is immediate and compliance is demonstrated.
Is capital adequacy reviewed during SEBI inspections?
Yes. Net worth is a key inspection checkpoint.
Is client eligibility verification mandatory?
Yes. PMS must onboard only eligible clients meeting prescribed investment thresholds.
Is a written PMS agreement compulsory?
Yes. Every client relationship must be governed by a written portfolio management agreement.
Is risk profiling mandatory for PMS clients?
Yes. Suitability and risk assessment are essential compliance requirements.
Can PMS onboard clients without completing documentation?
No. Incomplete onboarding is a serious compliance violation.
Are changes in client mandate allowed?
Yes, but only with proper documentation and client consent.
Must client funds be segregated from PMS proprietary funds?
Yes. Strict segregation of client and proprietary assets is mandatory.
Can PMS pool client funds?
Only as permitted under SEBI regulations and disclosed structures.
Is commingling of funds allowed?
No. Commingling is treated as a major compliance failure.
Are separate bank and demat accounts required?
Yes. Proper account segregation must be maintained.
Is asset segregation reviewed during inspections?
Yes. This is a key focus area for SEBI.
Can PMS deviate from disclosed investment strategy?
No. Any deviation requires client consent and disclosure.
Are investment restrictions binding?
Yes. PMS must adhere to permitted investment limits and disclosures.
Can PMS guarantee returns?
No. Return assurance is strictly prohibited.
Is leverage permitted in PMS?
Only within permitted regulatory boundaries and disclosures.
Can PMS trade in unapproved instruments?
No. Investments must align with disclosed strategy and regulatory norms.
Is periodic performance reporting mandatory?
Yes. Clients must receive regular portfolio performance reports.
Must PMS disclose benchmark performance?
Yes. Benchmark comparison is a key disclosure requirement.
Can PMS selectively disclose performance data?
No. Selective or misleading disclosure is prohibited.
Is methodology disclosure required?
Yes. Performance calculation methodology must be transparent.
Are past returns allowed in marketing material?
Only in a factual, compliant, and non-misleading manner.
Must PMS disclose fee structure clearly?
Yes. All fees and expenses must be transparently disclosed.
Can PMS charge fees beyond agreement terms?
No. Fees must strictly follow the client agreement.
Is performance fee subject to conditions?
Yes. Performance fees must comply with disclosed terms and regulatory norms.
Are hidden charges permitted?
No. Hidden or ambiguous charges are prohibited.
Can PMS revise fees mid-term?
Only with client consent and proper disclosure.
Is appointment of a Compliance Officer mandatory?
Yes. A designated Compliance Officer is compulsory.
Can the Compliance Officer be outsourced?
Certain functions may be outsourced, but responsibility remains with the PMS.
Is Board oversight expected for PMS compliance?
Yes. Board and senior management oversight is required.
Are internal audits required?
Yes. Internal compliance reviews are expected periodically.
Can compliance failures impact registration validity?
Yes. Serious or repeated failures can lead to suspension or cancellation.
Are periodic reports to SEBI mandatory?
Yes. PMS entities must submit prescribed reports within timelines.
Is delay in reporting treated seriously?
Yes. Delays attract regulatory scrutiny.
Must changes in key personnel be disclosed?
Yes. Changes in Principal Officer or Compliance Officer require immediate intimation.
Is prior approval required for change in control?
Yes. Prior SEBI approval is mandatory.
Can PMS continue operations during approval process?
Only as permitted under regulatory guidance.
Can SEBI conduct inspections without notice?
Yes. Inspections may be announced or unannounced.
Are inspection observations binding?
Yes. Corrective actions must be implemented within timelines.
Can PMS challenge inspection findings?
Clarifications may be provided, but compliance is mandatory.
What is the most common PMS compliance failure?
Weak asset segregation and inadequate documentation.
Is compliance a one-time exercise?
No. PMS compliance is continuous and evolving.
Does strong compliance improve investor confidence?
Yes. Compliance directly impacts credibility and trust.
Can good compliance reduce regulatory risk?
Absolutely. Structured compliance reduces inspection exposure.
Is PMS compliance expensive?
Compliance is an investment in business stability.
Can PMS outsource operations fully?
No. Regulatory responsibility always rests with the registered entity.
What is the safest compliance approach for PMS?
Clear processes, regular reviews, and professional regulatory support.
