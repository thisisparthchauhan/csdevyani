SEBI Registration

Portfolio Manager Registration
in India

A mandatory SEBI approval for entities intending to manage client portfolios. PMS is a high-responsibility, high-trust fiduciary activity.

Start Registration Process

This registration is designed for serious market participants intending to build a long-term, regulated investment management business. A registered Portfolio Manager does not merely advise — it assumes fiduciary responsibility.

Who Should Apply?

PMS registration is suitable for entities looking to offer discretionary or non-discretionary portfolio management services.

  • Investment professionals seeking to manage client wealth
  • Asset managers targeting HNI and accredited investors
  • Fund managers transitioning from advisory or AIF structures
  • Institutions setting up India-based or GIFT City PMS platforms

What Is a Portfolio Manager?

A body corporate that manages or administers a portfolio of securities or funds on behalf of clients, under a written agreement.

Discretionary PMS

Decisions taken by the Portfolio Manager.

Non-Discretionary PMS

Decisions executed based on client instructions.

Eligibility Criteria

SEBI evaluates both institutional capability and individual competence.

1. Constitution

Must be a Body Corporate. Individuals, partnerships, or proprietorships are not eligible.

2. Minimum Net Worth

₹5 Crore

Must be maintained at all times. Certified by a Chartered Accountant. Not a one-time condition.

3. Principal Officer

Professional qualification (Finance/Law/CA/MBA) + 5 Years Exp + NISM PMS Certification.

4. Compliance Officer

Designated officer responsible for regulatory compliance and SEBI reporting.

5. Infrastructure

Adequate office, IT systems, and manpower to handle portfolio management activities.

Registration Process

Step 1

Readiness Assessment

Detailed evaluation of net worth, personnel qualifications, governance framework, and operational readiness.

Step 2

Application Filing

Online submission via SEBI Intermediary Portal (Form A) with support docs and fee payment.

Step 3

SEBI Review & Clarifications

SEBI may seek additional info, explanations, or meetings. Tests seriousness and compliance mindset.

Step 4

Grant of Registration

Upon satisfaction, Registration Certificate is issued. Valid unless suspended/cancelled.

What a Registered PMS Can Do

Onboard eligible clientsManage discretionary/non-discretionary portfoliosInvest across permitted securitiesCharge management & performance feesDisclose & benchmark performance

Continuous Supervision

Registration confers authority; compliance sustains legitimacy. SEBI expects strong fiduciary responsibility from day one.

PMS Registration – FAQs

Expert answers to common queries on Portfolio Manager Registration.

What is PMS registration?
PMS registration is approval granted by SEBI to an entity to manage client portfolios of securities or funds under a formal portfolio management arrangement.
Who regulates Portfolio Managers in India?
Portfolio Managers are regulated by the Securities and Exchange Board of India.
Is PMS registration mandatory?
Yes. Any entity managing client portfolios for consideration must obtain PMS registration from SEBI.
Can PMS operate without SEBI registration?
No. Operating without registration is a serious regulatory violation.
What is the core role of a Portfolio Manager?
A Portfolio Manager manages investments on behalf of clients and acts as a fiduciary.
Who is eligible to apply for PMS registration?
Only a body corporate is eligible to apply for PMS registration.
Can individuals apply for PMS registration?
No. Individuals, proprietorships, or partnerships are not eligible.
Can an LLP apply for PMS registration?
No. PMS registration is granted only to companies.
Can a foreign company apply for PMS registration in India?
Foreign entities must establish an eligible Indian entity to apply.
Is prior experience in capital markets mandatory?
Yes. SEBI expects relevant experience at the management level.
What is the minimum net worth required for PMS registration?
A minimum net worth of ₹5 crore is mandatory.
Is net worth requirement a one-time condition?
No. The net worth must be maintained at all times.
How is net worth calculated for PMS?
Net worth includes paid-up capital and free reserves, excluding revaluation reserves.
Can borrowed funds be used to meet net worth?
No. Net worth must be owned funds.
Is net worth certification required?
Yes. Net worth must be certified by a Chartered Accountant.
Who is a Principal Officer in PMS?
The Principal Officer is responsible for investment decisions and regulatory compliance.
Is qualification mandatory for Principal Officer?
Yes. Professional qualification and relevant experience are required.
Is NISM certification mandatory for PMS?
Yes. The Principal Officer must hold the prescribed NISM certification.
Is a Compliance Officer mandatory?
Yes. Appointment of a Compliance Officer is compulsory.
Can the same person be Principal Officer and Compliance Officer?
Generally discouraged; segregation of roles is preferred.
How is PMS registration application filed?
The application is filed through SEBI’s intermediary portal in the prescribed form.
Is the application fee refundable?
No. The application fee is non-refundable.
Does SEBI ask for clarifications during processing?
Yes. SEBI may seek explanations, documents, or meetings.
How long does PMS registration take?
Timelines vary based on completeness and regulatory review.
Can SEBI reject a PMS application?
Yes. Applications not meeting regulatory expectations may be rejected.
What services can a registered PMS provide?
Managing discretionary or non-discretionary portfolios of eligible clients.
Can PMS provide investment advisory services?
Yes, but within PMS regulatory framework and disclosures.
Can PMS manage client funds directly?
PMS manages portfolios, not pooled funds like mutual funds.
Can PMS guarantee returns?
No. Guaranteeing returns is strictly prohibited.
Can PMS manage portfolios of retail investors?
Only clients meeting prescribed minimum investment thresholds can be onboarded.
Is a written agreement with clients mandatory?
Yes. A Portfolio Management Agreement must be executed.
Is minimum investment required from PMS clients?
Yes. SEBI prescribes a minimum investment threshold.
Is risk profiling mandatory for PMS clients?
Yes. Client suitability must be assessed and documented.
Can PMS onboard clients without KYC?
No. KYC compliance is mandatory.
Can PMS change client mandate later?
Yes, with client consent and proper documentation.
Can PMS charge management fees?
Yes. Fees must be disclosed and agreed contractually.
Can PMS charge performance fees?
Yes, subject to regulatory conditions and disclosures.
Are hidden charges allowed in PMS?
No. All charges must be transparently disclosed.
Can PMS revise fees during the term?
Only with client consent and proper documentation.
Is fee sharing with distributors allowed?
Only within regulatory framework and disclosures.
Does PMS registration involve ongoing compliance?
Yes. PMS entities are continuously regulated.
Is SEBI inspection applicable to PMS?
Yes. PMS entities are subject to regulatory inspections.
Can PMS registration be suspended?
Yes. Serious non-compliance may lead to suspension or cancellation.
Is change in control allowed after registration?
Yes, but only with prior SEBI approval.
Are changes in directors required to be reported?
Yes. Key changes must be disclosed to SEBI.
Is PMS registration suitable for fund managers?
Yes. PMS is a preferred structure for HNI-focused fund managers.
Can PMS be operated from GIFT City?
Yes, subject to applicable regulatory framework.
Can PMS convert into an AIF later?
Yes, but separate registration is required.
Is PMS registration transferable?
No. Registration is entity-specific and non-transferable.
Is PMS registration permanent?
Registration remains valid unless suspended, cancelled, or surrendered.
What is the biggest challenge in PMS registration?
Meeting net worth, governance, and compliance expectations together.
What is the safest approach for PMS registration?
Strong compliance planning, experienced professionals, and regulatory-aligned structuring.
