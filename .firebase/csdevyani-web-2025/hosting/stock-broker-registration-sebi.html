Stock Broker Registration
SEBI

Mandatory statutory requirement for entities intending to deal in securities. No exchange membership or business arrangement can substitute SEBI registration.

Start Application Process

Acting as a stock broker without SEBI registration is illegal, irrespective of whether trades are proprietary or client-based.

Who Must Obtain Registration?

A "stock broker" is a member of a recognised stock exchange permitted to deal in securities. Registration is compulsory for:

  • Members of recognised stock exchanges
  • Proprietary trading members
  • Trading members executing client trades
  • Entities clearing or settling trades on behalf of clients

Broker Categories

Trading Member (TM)

Executes trades on exchange; clearing via CM/PCM.

Self-Clearing Member (SCM)

Executes and clears own trades.

Clearing Member (CM)

Clears and settles trades for self and others.

Professional Clearing Member (PCM)

Dedicated to clearing services; prohibited from trading.

Eligibility & Financial Requirements

SEBI examines eligibility on objective parameters, not business intent.

Core Eligibility Criteria

Legal Constitution

Individual, Partnership, LLP, or Corporate entity aligning with exchange bye-laws.

Fit and Proper

Integrity, good reputation, no economic offences, and financial soundness.

Infrastructure

Office premises, trading terminals, connectivity, and risk management systems.

Qualified Personnel

Compliance Officer, Authorized Persons, and certified dealers.

Minimum Net Worth

CategoryMin. Net Worth
Trading Member (TM)₹1 Crore
Self-Clearing Member (SCM)₹2 Crore
Clearing Member (CM)₹3 Crore
Professional Clearing Member (PCM)₹8 Crore

Note:

Net worth must be maintained on a continuous basis, not just at the time of registration.

Application Process – Step by Step

1

Exchange Membership

Obtain membership of recognised stock exchange (NSE/BSE/MSEI). Exchange approval is a pre-condition to SEBI registration.

2

Application to SEBI (Form A)

Submit application via the exchange with constitutional docs, net worth certs, and infrastructure details.

3

Regulatory Scrutiny

SEBI/Exchange may seek clarifications, additional docs, or personal interaction. Satisfaction is discretionary.

4

Grant of Registration

On satisfaction, SEBI issues Certificate of Registration (Form D). Specific to exchange membership.

What a Registered Broker Can Do

Execute trades on exchangesUndertake proprietary tradingAppoint Authorised PersonsAccess clearing systemsEarn brokerage income

Continuous Supervision

Registration does not end regulatory responsibility. It is the beginning of continuous supervision through inspections, audits, and investor grievance monitoring.

Stock Broker Registration – FAQs

Common questions about becoming a SEBI Registered Stock Broker.

What is Stock Broker Registration with SEBI?
It is the statutory registration required to act as a stock broker in India and execute trades on recognised stock exchanges. Without SEBI registration, broking activity is not permitted.
Who is a stock broker as per SEBI?
A stock broker is a SEBI-registered intermediary who is also a member of a recognised stock exchange and is permitted to trade on exchange platforms.
Is stock exchange membership enough to start broking?
No. Exchange membership and SEBI registration are linked but distinct. Trading as a broker is permitted only after SEBI issues the registration certificate.
Can I run a broking business without SEBI registration if I only do proprietary trading?
No. Proprietary trading as a "member" on exchange platform also requires registration under the applicable broker category.
Is SEBI registration compulsory for sub-brokers or authorised persons?
Authorised Persons operate under a registered stock broker. They do not take separate SEBI broker registration, but must be empanelled through the broker and comply with exchange norms.
What is the difference between stock broker and investment adviser?
A stock broker executes trades and provides broking services; an investment adviser provides regulated advice. Their registrations, permissions, and compliances are separate.
Can a stock broker also be a research analyst or investment adviser?
Yes, but only with separate registrations (if applicable) and strict segregation of activities, teams, conflicts, and disclosures.
What is the legal framework for stock brokers in India?
Stock brokers are governed under the SEBI Act, SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations, exchange bye-laws, clearing corporation rules, and SEBI/exchange circulars.
What is SEBI "fit and proper" criteria for brokers?
It is a suitability test covering integrity, reputation, competence, financial soundness, and clean regulatory track record of promoters/directors/partners.
Can SEBI reject an application even if documents are submitted?
Yes. Registration is granted only if SEBI is satisfied about eligibility, fit and proper status, infrastructure, and compliance readiness.
Who can apply for Stock Broker Registration (SEBI)?
Individuals, partnership firms, LLPs, and companies can apply, subject to exchange membership eligibility and SEBI fit-and-proper requirements.
Can a new company apply for broker registration?
Yes, a newly incorporated entity can apply if it meets membership requirements, net worth, governance, infrastructure, and compliance arrangements.
Is prior experience mandatory to become a stock broker?
Experience is considered positively, but eligibility is primarily based on compliance with regulatory and exchange admission criteria.
What background checks are done for broker registration?
Checks commonly include criminal history, market-related disciplinary actions, exchange defaulter lists, financial credibility, and regulatory proceedings.
Does SEBI check directors/partners individually?
Yes. Key persons and control persons are assessed for fit and proper status, including past disciplinary history.
Can a partnership firm become a broker?
Yes, if the exchange permits, partners meet eligibility criteria, and the firm fulfils net worth and infrastructure conditions.
Can an LLP become a stock broker?
Yes, subject to exchange admission and SEBI requirements, including governance, designated partners, and compliance structure.
Can an individual become a stock broker?
Yes, subject to exchange membership conditions and SEBI registration requirements. Practically, many brokers prefer corporate structure due to scale and compliance needs.
Is there an age requirement to become a broker?
For individuals and key persons, exchanges typically require adulthood and legal capacity to contract; specific membership criteria apply as per exchange rules.
Is there a qualification requirement for directors or key staff?
Key personnel must meet exchange/SEBI certification requirements and competency norms; dealers and associated persons often require prescribed market certifications.
What are the common broker categories on exchanges?
Trading Member (TM), Self-Clearing Member (SCM), Clearing Member (CM), and Professional Clearing Member (PCM) are commonly used classifications.
What is a Trading Member (TM)?
A TM executes trades on the exchange. Clearing and settlement may be through a clearing member unless the TM is self-clearing.
What is a Clearing Member (CM)?
A CM clears and settles trades, manages margins, and interfaces with clearing corporation obligations, subject to higher financial requirements.
What is a Self-Clearing Member (SCM)?
An SCM executes trades and clears/settles its own trades without depending on another clearing member.
What is a Professional Clearing Member (PCM)?
A PCM primarily provides clearing services for other trading members and has higher net worth and operational responsibilities.
Can I start as a trading member and later become a clearing member?
Yes, subject to meeting enhanced requirements, approvals, and migration process as per the exchange and clearing corporation.
What is a discount broker model?
It is a commercial model where brokerage is lower or flat-fee; however, the regulatory obligations remain the same.
Can a broker offer both equity and derivatives?
Yes, but segment-wise exchange approvals, net worth, deposits, and system readiness must be fulfilled.
Can a broker offer commodity trading also?
Commodity broking requires membership on commodity segments/exchanges and adherence to segment-specific requirements and approvals.
Can a broker offer DP services (demat accounts) automatically?
No. DP registration is a separate approval under depository regulations and depository bye-laws, with separate net worth and operational conditions.
What is net worth requirement for SEBI stock broker registration?
Net worth depends on broker category and segment (TM/SCM/CM/PCM) and is prescribed by exchanges/clearing corporations along with SEBI framework.
How is net worth calculated for broker eligibility?
Net worth is typically computed as paid-up capital plus free reserves minus intangible assets and other specified deductions, as per regulatory/exchange computation norms.
Is continuous net worth maintenance compulsory after registration?
Yes. Net worth is not a one-time condition. Continuous maintenance is expected, and shortfall can trigger restrictions.
What is the difference between net worth and deposit with clearing corporation?
Net worth is the firm’s financial strength; deposits (base/IFSC/SGF/other margins) are risk buffers placed with clearing corporation/exchange.
Are exchange deposits refundable?
Deposits are generally refundable subject to settlement of all liabilities and formal exit/surrender process.
Do brokers need to maintain liquid assets?
Yes, exchanges/clearing corporations often prescribe liquid asset components for meeting margin and risk requirements.
Can net worth be funded through loans?
Net worth must be genuine and compliant with computation norms; artificial inflation through non-permissible funding may be questioned during scrutiny.
Does SEBI prescribe a single fixed capital amount for all brokers?
No. Requirements vary by broker type, segment, and exchange/clearing corporation norms.
Will SEBI verify bank statements and financials?
Yes, financial capacity is examined through audited financials, net worth certificates, and supporting evidence.
What if net worth falls below requirement after registration?
The broker may face restrictions, enhanced monitoring, or membership suspension until the deficiency is rectified.
What is the first step for Stock Broker Registration (SEBI)?
The first step is to apply for membership with the recognised stock exchange in the intended segment.
Is SEBI registration filed directly by the applicant?
Typically, the application is routed through the stock exchange as per prescribed process and forms.
What documents are generally required for broker registration?
Constitutional documents, KYC of promoters/directors, net worth proof, infrastructure details, compliance setup, experience details, and declarations.
Do I need office premises to apply?
Yes. Adequate office space, systems, connectivity, and manpower readiness are generally verified.
Is a technology vendor mandatory for broker operations?
Brokers must use approved trading and risk systems. The exact approach depends on exchange-approved technology options.
How long does the registration process take?
It depends on completeness of documentation, exchange processing time, SEBI scrutiny, and query resolution.
What are common reasons for application delays?
Incomplete documents, fit-and-proper clarifications, net worth computation issues, inadequate infrastructure, and unresolved exchange queries.
Can SEBI raise queries after submission?
Yes. SEBI and exchanges may raise detailed queries and request clarifications, undertakings, or revised documents.
What certificate is issued after approval?
SEBI issues a Certificate of Registration as a stock broker, subject to terms and conditions.
Can registration be transferred to another entity?
No. Registration is not transferable. Change in control requires approvals; transfer typically needs structured regulatory process.
What fees are payable for becoming a stock broker?
Costs typically include exchange admission fees, SEBI application/registration fees, deposits to clearing corporation, and technology/compliance setup costs.
Are SEBI application fees refundable if rejected?
Generally, application fees are non-refundable.
Are there annual recurring charges after registration?
Yes. There are recurring charges like exchange fees, clearing charges, technology costs, audits, and compliance-related expenses.
Does a stock broker need professional indemnity insurance?
Depending on business model and exchange expectations, risk insurance may be advisable; some frameworks require specific coverage.
Is GST applicable on brokerage and fees?
Yes, GST applicability is subject to prevailing tax laws and invoicing structure.
Can a broker appoint branches and franchise offices?
Yes, but branches and AP/franchise arrangements must be permitted, documented, and compliant with exchange rules.
Can a broker operate pan-India?
Yes, subject to compliance with branch/AP requirements and operational capability.
Is investor grievance mechanism compulsory from day one?
Yes. A documented grievance redressal system is expected from commencement.
Can a broker provide margin funding?
Only if permitted under applicable regulations and exchange framework, with proper risk controls and disclosures.
What is the safest way to structure a new broking set-up?
A regulator-aligned structure includes strong governance, clean segregation of client funds, robust RMS, auditable systems, and a compliance-first operating model.
