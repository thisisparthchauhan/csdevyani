for SEBI Research Analysts

Continuous regulatory accountability, independence, and documented discipline for registered Research Analysts in India.

From Registration to Accountability

Once SEBI Research Analyst Registration is granted, the role of a Research Analyst transitions from eligibility to continuous regulatory accountability. SEBI expects registered Research Analysts to operate with independence, transparency, and documented discipline at all times.

Non-compliance post-registration is one of the most common triggers for inspections, warnings, and enforcement actions by the Securities and Exchange Board of India.

Code of Conduct & Ethical Obligations

Foundational principles for registered Research Analysts

Honesty & Fairness

Acting honestly and fairly in preparation and dissemination of research

Objectivity & Independence

Ensuring objectivity and independence of opinions

Avoid Misleading Content

Avoiding exaggerated, misleading, or selective recommendations

Due Diligence

Exercising due diligence before issuing any research output

Research must be driven by analysis, not incentives.

Independence of Research & Conflict Management

Independence Requirements

  • Complete independence between research and any other commercial activity
  • No direct or indirect influence from issuers, promoters, or intermediaries
  • Clear identification and management of conflicts of interest

Conflict Management Process

1

Identified in advance

2

Disclosed prominently

3

Managed through internal controls

Critical Compliance Point

Failure to disclose conflicts is treated as a serious compliance breach and may result in penalties or suspension.

Mandatory Disclosures in Research Reports

Every research report, recommendation, or trading call must include:

Research Analyst registration number

MANDATORY

Name of the analyst or entity issuing the research

MANDATORY

Nature of recommendation (buy / sell / hold / view)

MANDATORY

Time horizon and risks associated

MANDATORY

Disclosure of personal or institutional interest, if any

MANDATORY

Disclosures must be clear, legible, and not buried in fine print.

Trading Restrictions & Personal Dealings

Strict Personal Trading Restrictions

No Pre-Publication Trading

Analysts cannot trade in securities before publication of research

Cooling-Off Periods

Mandatory waiting periods before and after recommendations

Pre-Clearance Required

Internal approvals may be required for personal trades

Documentation Mandatory

All personal trades must be documented and maintained

These restrictions are designed to prevent front-running and misuse of information.

Maintenance of Records & Documentation

Research Analysts must maintain detailed records for regulatory compliance and inspection readiness.

Record TypeMinimum Retention Period
Research reports & rationale5 years
Data sources & analysis notes5 years
Disclosures & disclaimers5 years
Client communication5 years
Personal trading records5 years
Complaints & grievance records5 years

Note: Records may be maintained electronically, provided they are secure, retrievable, and tamper-proof.

Client Onboarding & Communication Controls

For paid research services, the following controls must be in place:

Client Documentation

Clear client onboarding documentation must be maintained

Scope Definition

Scope of research services must be defined

No Return Assurance

No assurance of returns may be given

Consistent Communication

All communications must remain consistent with published research

Selective sharing of recommendations is strictly prohibited.

Website & Digital Platform Compliance

Registered Research Analysts operating websites, apps, Telegram channels, or social platforms must ensure:

Registration details are prominently displayed

Disclosures are visible on all platforms

Historical performance is not misrepresented

Testimonials or guarantees are not used improperly

Digital reach does not dilute regulatory responsibility.

Advertisement & Public Communication Restrictions

Research Analysts Must NOT:

Advertise guaranteed returns

Use promotional language inconsistent with research nature

Publish selective or cherry-picked performance results

Mislead investors through marketing claims

Any public communication must be educational and factual, not promotional.

Grievance Redressal Mechanism

Every Research Analyst must establish:

Formal Mechanism

A formal grievance redressal mechanism

Designated Contact

Designated contact details for complaints

Timely Resolution

Timely resolution of investor grievances

Complaint Registers

Maintenance of complaint registers

Unresolved complaints may escalate to SEBI and invite inspection.

Periodic Reporting & Regulatory Intimations

Research Analysts are required to:

Submit periodic compliance confirmations as prescribed

Intimate changes in business model, control, or key personnel

Report material events impacting research independence

Timely reporting reflects regulatory maturity and governance strength.

Annual Compliance Audit

Who Conducts the Audit

Every registered Research Analyst must undergo an annual compliance audit conducted by:

  • Practising Chartered Accountant (CA)
  • Company Secretary (CS)
  • Cost Accountant (CMA)

What is Examined

The audit examines:

  • Adherence to SEBI Research Analyst Regulations
  • Disclosure practices
  • Trading restrictions
  • Record maintenance
  • Conflict management

Audit observations must be addressed promptly to maintain compliance status.

SEBI Inspection & Enforcement Readiness

SEBI May Conduct Inspections:

With or Without Prior Notice

Inspections can be surprise visits

Comprehensive Coverage

Records, systems, and communications

Digital Content Included

Including archived material

Research Analysts must cooperate fully and maintain inspection-ready documentation at all times.

Consequences of Non-Compliance

Failure to comply may result in:

Monetary Penalties

Significant fines as per SEBI regulations

Directions or Restrictions

Operational restrictions imposed by SEBI

Suspension or Cancellation

Loss of registration and ability to operate

Reputational Damage

Loss of credibility and client trust

Post-registration compliance is therefore as critical as registration itself.

FAQs – SEBI Research Analyst Registration & Compliance

Common questions regarding post-registration obligations.

1. What is SEBI Research Analyst registration?
SEBI Research Analyst registration is a mandatory approval required for individuals or entities providing research reports, stock recommendations, price targets, or trading opinions related to securities.
2. Who is required to obtain Research Analyst registration?
Any person or organisation issuing research opinions or recommendations for consideration must obtain registration before commencing such activities.
3. Can someone publish research reports without SEBI registration?
No. Publishing or distributing research without registration is treated as unauthorised activity and may attract regulatory action.
4. Which regulations govern Research Analysts in India?
Research Analysts are governed under the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014, along with subsequent amendments and circulars.
5. What qualifications are required to become a Research Analyst?
Applicants must meet prescribed educational requirements and hold a valid NISM Research Analyst certification.
6. Is a finance degree compulsory for Research Analyst registration?
A finance degree is not compulsory if the applicant holds the prescribed certification and meets eligibility norms.
7. What certification is mandatory for Research Analysts?
A valid NISM Research Analyst certification, as prescribed by SEBI, is mandatory.
8. What does “fit and proper” criteria mean for Research Analysts?
It refers to integrity, reputation, financial soundness, and regulatory track record of the applicant and key persons.
9. Is a security deposit required after registration?
Yes. Research Analysts must maintain a security deposit based on the size of their client base.
10. How is the security deposit amount decided?
The amount depends on the number of clients served by the Research Analyst.
11. Where is the Research Analyst application submitted?
The application is submitted to SEBI in the prescribed format along with supporting documents and fees.
12. Can foreign entities register as Research Analysts in India?
Foreign entities generally cannot directly register; research for Indian clients must be routed through a registered entity.
13. What documents are required for Research Analyst registration?
Documents include application forms, qualification proofs, certification details, business plan, and internal policies.
14. Is physical or IT infrastructure evaluated by SEBI?
Yes. SEBI assesses whether the applicant has adequate infrastructure to conduct research activities responsibly.
15. Is appointment of a Principal Officer mandatory?
Yes, for non-individual applicants, a Principal Officer must be designated to oversee compliance.
16. Can a Research Analyst distribute financial products?
No. Research Analysts are prohibited from distribution unless separately registered under applicable regulations.
17. Can one entity hold both Research Analyst and Investment Adviser registrations?
Yes, but strict segregation of activities, teams, and disclosures must be maintained.
18. Are Research Analysts allowed to charge fees?
Yes, Research Analysts may charge fees, subject to transparency and disclosure requirements.
19. Is advance fee collection permitted?
Advance fees are permitted within regulatory limits and subject to refund and disclosure norms.
20. Does Research Analyst registration require renewal every year?
Registration remains valid unless suspended or cancelled, but ongoing compliance and fee obligations apply.
21. Are conflict of interest policies mandatory?
Yes. Research Analysts must have documented conflict management policies.
22. What disclosures are mandatory in research reports?
Disclosures include registration details, analyst identity, conflicts of interest, and compensation links.
23. Are Research Analysts allowed to advertise their services?
Promotional activities are restricted and must not be misleading or solicit public investments.
24. Are there restrictions on personal trading by Research Analysts?
Yes. Personal trading must comply with internal policies to prevent misuse of information.
25. Is continuous professional education required?
Research Analysts must maintain valid certifications and stay updated with regulatory changes.
26. Is website compliance mandatory for Research Analysts?
Yes. Registration details and disclosures must be prominently displayed on websites and digital platforms.
27. Can Research Analysts publish research on social media platforms?
Yes, provided all disclosures and registration details accompany such publications.
28. How does SEBI monitor Research Analyst compliance?
SEBI monitors compliance through reporting, audits, inspections, and grievance mechanisms.
29. What records must Research Analysts maintain?
Records of research reports, data sources, disclosures, client communications, and trades must be preserved.
30. Is an annual compliance audit mandatory?
Yes. Research Analysts must undergo an annual compliance audit by a qualified professional.
31. Are audit observations reportable to SEBI?
Material audit findings must be addressed and reported as required.
32. Can SEBI amend Research Analyst regulations?
Yes. SEBI may amend regulations or issue clarifications from time to time.
33. Does the number of clients affect compliance requirements?
Yes. Client volume impacts security deposit and reporting obligations.
34. Why is a security deposit required?
The deposit acts as financial assurance for investor protection and regulatory enforcement.
35. Is membership of a supervisory body required?
Yes. Research Analysts must be associated with a recognised supervisory body.
36. Can compliance functions be outsourced?
Compliance functions may be outsourced, but responsibility remains with the Research Analyst.
37. How long does the registration process usually take?
Timelines vary based on documentation quality and regulatory clarifications.
38. Can a Research Analyst refuse to onboard a client?
Yes. Research Analysts may refuse engagements that conflict with regulations or independence.
39. Are Research Analysts allowed to analyse complex products?
Yes, provided adequate expertise and disclosures are maintained.
40. Is a separate bank account required for research activities?
Maintaining a separate account is considered a good governance practice.
41. Can SEBI suspend Research Analyst registration?
Yes. SEBI may suspend or cancel registration for serious non-compliance.
42. Is client consent required for disclosures?
Clients must acknowledge disclosures before availing research services.
43. Can Research Analysts provide investment advice?
No. Investment advice requires separate registration as an Investment Adviser.
44. Are financial projections allowed in research reports?
Yes, provided assumptions and risks are clearly explained.
45. Is there a cap on Research Analyst fees?
Fees must be reasonable, transparent, and fairly disclosed.
46. Can unregistered individuals assist in research preparation?
Only registered Research Analysts can issue research under their name.
47. Can Research Analysts run paid newsletters or subscriptions?
Yes, subject to full compliance with disclosure and conduct norms.
48. Are Research Analysts liable for misleading research?
Yes. Misleading or biased research may result in regulatory penalties.
49. Is a grievance redressal mechanism mandatory?
Yes. Research Analysts must maintain a formal grievance redressal process.
50. How can Research Analysts ensure long-term compliance?
By maintaining disciplined disclosures, proper records, internal controls, and regulatory awareness.
