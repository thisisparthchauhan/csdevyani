Depository Participant
Registration in India

Mandatory approval for entities intending to provide demat account services. Start your journey as a SEBI-registered Depository Participant (DP) with expert guidance.

Start Your Application

A Depository Participant (DP) operates in a highly regulated ecosystem, handling investor securities and settlement services. Given the sensitivity, SEBI follows a strict eligibility and governance-based approach.

What Is a Depository Participant (DP)?

A Depository Participant is an entity registered with SEBI and admitted by a depository (NSDL/CDSL), authorised to act as an interface between the depository and beneficial owners.

  • Open and maintain demat accounts
  • Facilitate dematerialisation & rematerialisation
  • Execute settlement-related instructions
  • Act as a point of service for investors

Eligible Applicants

Scheduled Commercial Banks
Public Financial Institutions
Custodians registered with SEBI
Clearing Corporations / Houses
Stock Brokers (Conditions apply)
Certain NBFCs & RTAs

Eligibility Criteria

Constitution

Must be a legally recognised entity (Company, Bank, PFI). Individuals & Partnerships are NOT eligible.

Net Worth

Stock Brokers: ₹3 Cr (Initial) → ₹5 Cr. RTAs: ₹10 Cr. NBFCs: ₹50 Lakh (Restricted).

Fit & Proper

Applicant, Promoters, Directors & KMPs must have clean regulatory track record and financial integrity.

Infrastructure

Secure IT systems, access controls, BCP/DR, and trained manpower are mandatory.

Registration Process

Step-by-step roadmap to obtaining your DP license.

1
01

Application Through Depository

Submit Form F via NSDL/CDSL with infrastructure, net worth, and compliance disclosures.

2
02

Depository Review

Depository examines eligibility, infrastructure readiness, and operational capability.

3
03

SEBI Scrutiny

SEBI reviews the application, focuses on risk management, and may seek clarifications.

4
04

Grant of Certificate

Upon satisfaction, SEBI grants the Certificate of Registration.

DP Registration – FAQs

Expert answers to common queries on becoming a Depository Participant.

What is DP Registration?
DP Registration is approval granted to an entity to act as a Depository Participant and provide demat account services to investors.
Who regulates Depository Participants in India?
Depository Participants are regulated by the Securities and Exchange Board of India in coordination with depositories.
Is DP registration mandatory to open demat accounts?
Yes. No entity can open or operate demat accounts without DP registration.
What is the role of a Depository Participant?
A DP acts as the interface between the depository and investors for holding and transferring securities in electronic form.
Can a DP hold securities in its own name?
No. Securities are held in the name of the beneficial owner.
Who is eligible to apply for DP registration?
Only SEBI-approved categories such as stock brokers, banks, RTAs, custodians, and certain NBFCs are eligible.
Can an individual apply for DP registration?
No. DP registration is granted only to eligible institutional entities.
Can a partnership firm apply for DP registration?
No. Partnership firms are not eligible.
Can an LLP apply for DP registration?
Generally no, unless specifically permitted under regulatory norms.
Can a foreign entity apply for DP registration?
Foreign entities must establish a compliant Indian entity to apply.
Is minimum net worth required for DP registration?
Yes. Minimum net worth depends on the category of applicant.
What is the net worth requirement for stock broker DPs?
Stock brokers must meet prescribed minimum net worth thresholds and enhancement timelines.
Is net worth requirement a one-time condition?
No. Net worth must be maintained continuously.
Can borrowed funds be used to meet net worth?
No. Net worth must consist of owned funds.
Is CA certification required for net worth?
Yes. Net worth must be certified by a Chartered Accountant.
Is physical office infrastructure mandatory?
Yes. Adequate office and operational infrastructure is required.
Are IT systems mandatory for DP registration?
Yes. Secure IT systems and connectivity with the depository are essential.
Is disaster recovery setup required at registration stage?
Yes. BCP and DR preparedness are evaluated.
Is manpower strength evaluated by regulators?
Yes. Adequate trained staff is required.
Can infrastructure be outsourced?
Certain functions may be outsourced, but responsibility remains with the DP.
How is DP registration application made?
Application is submitted to the depository in the prescribed format.
Is the application submitted directly to SEBI?
No. The application is routed through the depository.
Is the application fee refundable?
No. Application fee is non-refundable.
Does SEBI seek clarifications during processing?
Yes. SEBI may seek additional information or explanations.
Can the application be rejected?
Yes. If eligibility or readiness is inadequate.
When is DP registration granted?
After depository recommendation and SEBI approval.
Is DP registration permanent?
It remains valid unless suspended, cancelled, or surrendered.
Can registration be surrendered voluntarily?
Yes, subject to regulatory approval and exit compliance.
Is renewal of registration required?
No periodic renewal is required, but annual fees apply.
Can registration be suspended?
Yes. For serious regulatory violations.
What activities can a registered DP undertake?
Opening demat accounts, dematerialisation, rematerialisation, pledging, and settlement services.
Can a DP offer trading services?
Only if separately registered as a stock broker.
Can DP handle corporate actions?
Yes, as part of depository services.
Can DP operate multiple branches?
Yes, subject to approval and control systems.
Can DP service both retail and institutional clients?
Yes.
Are DPs allowed to charge clients?
Yes, as per approved fee structures.
Are DP charges regulated?
Yes. Charges must comply with prescribed limits and disclosures.
Can DP revise charges?
Yes, with proper disclosure and compliance.
Are hidden charges permitted?
No. Transparency is mandatory.
Is DP required to display fee schedule?
Yes. Fee transparency is required.
Is appointment of Compliance Officer mandatory?
Yes. A Compliance Officer is required.
Are directors subject to fit and proper criteria?
Yes. Continuous fit and proper status is mandatory.
Is board oversight expected for DP operations?
Yes. Governance oversight is essential.
Are internal policies required at registration stage?
Yes. SOPs and policies demonstrate operational readiness.
Is client grievance mechanism mandatory?
Yes. Even at registration stage, a grievance framework must exist.
Is DP registration suitable for new businesses?
Yes, if eligibility and infrastructure requirements are met.
Is DP registration capital intensive?
Yes. DP registration requires financial and operational strength.
Can DP registration be converted into other SEBI registrations?
No. Separate registrations are required.
Is DP registration transferable?
No. Registration is entity-specific.
What is the biggest challenge in DP registration?
Meeting infrastructure, net worth, and governance requirements together.
What is the safest way to obtain DP registration?
Proper regulatory planning, readiness assessment, and professional guidance.
